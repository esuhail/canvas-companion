# Privacy

Canvas Companion has no server. Nothing it reads or stores leaves your browser.

## What it reads

Only while the side panel is open, and only from the one Canvas site you connected:

- your profile record, to confirm you are signed in
- your active courses and the totals Canvas reports
- each course's assignment groups, assignments, due dates and your own scores

It asks Canvas to leave out assignment descriptions and rubrics. It never reads quizzes, submissions, messages, files or other people's data, and it never sends anything to Canvas except read requests.

## What it stores, in this browser only

| Stored | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The Canvas address and which connection method worked | So you do not reconnect every time |
| Targets, what-if scores and work you added | Your own input |
| Weights and dated items read from a syllabus you supplied | The syllabus check. The syllabus file and text are not kept. |
| A snapshot of scores and due dates, keyed by ID, with no names | "Changed since your last visit" |

**Disconnect** erases all of it and hands the site permission back to Chrome.

## What it never does

- No account, analytics, ads or tracking.
- No requests to any site other than your Canvas.
- No background activity while the panel is closed.

## Optional on-device model

If your Chrome has its built-in on-device model and you tick the experimental box, syllabus text is also read by that model. It runs on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
